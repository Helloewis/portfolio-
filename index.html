About Me

From bench-level hardware tinkering to conceptual & structural design

I am Arya Tandon, a Mechanical Engineering student at Manav Rachna University. My engineering work spans both physical builds and conceptual system studies: packaging a compact sensor-based radar module, executing custom structural furniture builds from dimensional layout to assembly, and conceptualizing a 6-foot tree model engineered around VF-grade material to withstand varying temperatures.

Beyond mechanical structures, I regularly tinker with electronic hardware and perform component-level modifications on legacy audio devices—such as retrofitting classic iPods with upgraded storage and power architectures. I maintain moderate CAD proficiency, am actively learning Python, and aim to channel this multidisciplinary toolkit into Aerospace systems or Music engineering as an avid listener.
